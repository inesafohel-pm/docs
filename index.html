What is PaperPlane Solutions?
PPS processes real estate title documents (deeds, liens, taxes, judgments) using OCR and AI to extract structured data automatically. Instead of manually reading through hundreds of pages, the system scans documents, pulls out key data points, and generates ready-to-use reports — saving hours of manual review for real estate professionals.

For the POC, the entire flow runs through Google Drive.
